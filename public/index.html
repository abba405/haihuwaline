How it works

  1. 1
    Dial the code — from any basic phone, no internet needed.
  2. 2
    Choose what's happening and where — a short menu, no typing required.
  3. 3
    The nearest facility is alerted instantly — by SMS, with a call-back confirmation to the reporter.

Facility coverage

Reports from a listed ward route straight to that ward's facility. Any other location routes to the general coordination line, so no one is left without a contact.
